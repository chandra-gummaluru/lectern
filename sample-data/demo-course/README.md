A made-up, generic course offering (DEMO101, Fall 2026: "A Sample Course"), with 30 made-up students,
their marks and a midterm's cover page. Lectern's **Load the demo course** (Home) loads it as demo101 in
the current term, with its dates moved to that term (server/transfer.py). `pub/` is its public files
(course.json), `priv/` its private ones (gradebook, weeks, announcements, the midterm).
